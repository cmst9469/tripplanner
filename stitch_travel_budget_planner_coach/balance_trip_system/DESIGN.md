## Brand & Style

This design system embodies the balance between exploratory wanderlust and disciplined financial clarity. Designed for modern global travelers navigating itinerary density against strict spending envelopes, the brand balances premium hospitality restraint with agile personal finance utility.

### Identity & Tone
- **Curated & Grounded:** Evoking the quiet precision of premium architectural travel journals and refined hospitality editorial layouts.
- **Empathetic & Decisive:** Replacing financial anxiety with calm, contextual travel-budget insights and high-signal data visualization.
- **Atmospheric Elegance:** Warm stone and ivory paper-like backdrops create an organic canvas for rich metallic gold and earthen taupe touchpoints.

### Design Movement: Warm Editorial Modernism
The visual language fuses editorial hospitality layouts (structural ratios, generous white space, structured serif-like balance via DM Sans, and crisp tabular alignment) with warm, tactile card surfaces. Interfaces shun stark synthetic grays in favor of warm stone and mineral substrates, grounding data tables, itinerary timelines, and currency splits into an approachable, tactile travel planner.

## Colors

The color palette anchors the analytical depth of budget tracking with the warmth of journey exploration:

- **Primary (`#947229` / Antique Gold):** Used for authoritative wayfinding, brand markers, dominant navigation bars, primary table headers, and focused itinerary cards. Represents refined craftsmanship and reliable structure.
- **Secondary (`#877553` / Muted Taupe):** Reserved strictly for high-conversion CTAs, budget limit alerts, active booking states, and key interactive conversions.
- **Tertiary (`#A0B9EF` / Soft Periwinkle):** Signifies variable leisure spending, dining allocations, highlight badges, and caution-level budget alerts.
- **Neutral (`#7C766E` / Mineral Stone):** The foundational canvas. Provides the tactile warmth of luxury travel stationary, avoiding cold digital blues or stark whites.
- **Extended Categorical Palette:**
  - `surface-sand` (`#F4F0E8`): Nested container panels, sticky summary sheets, and table row zebra stripes.
  - `sky-tag` (`#86C5DA`): Transit, flight segments, and marine/coastal activity badges.
  - `sage-tag` (`#A8C6A0`): Lodging milestones, nature attractions, and balanced budget status confirmations.
  - `charcoal-text` (`#1A2421`): High-legibility deep forest-charcoal base for body text and numeric ledgers.
  - `muted-border` (`#E5DEC9`): Subtle boundary definition for layered content separation.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy prioritizes clarity for dual-currency financial ledgers, dense timeline itineraries, and high-level editorial headers.

- **Primary Family:** DM Sans brings geometric clarity combined with subtle humanist curves, delivering clean numeral alignment and compact horizontal rhythm across complex travel schedules.
- **Hierarchy Structure:**
  - `headline-xl` / `headline-lg`: Primary landing dashboards and daily destination overviews. Transitions gracefully down to mobile alternatives to prevent overflow in dual-currency price totals.
  - `headline-md` / `title-lg`: Card module titles, itinerary milestone anchors, and category budget summaries.
  - `body-lg` / `body-md`: Detailed trip notes, transit descriptions, and policy guidelines.
  - `label-*`: Used across timeline category tags, exchange rate tickers, tab bars, and tabular ledger headers. Formatted with slightly expanded letter tracking for immediate legibility at reduced sizes.

## Layout & Spacing

The system utilizes an asymmetrical 12-column responsive fluid grid on desktop (max width: 1280px) and a stacked 4-column framework on mobile devices, optimized for split-pane day planning (timeline on the left, budget telemetry on the right).

- **Grid Strategy:**
  - **Desktop (>= 1024px):** 12-column layout with `2rem` margin and `1.25rem` gutter. Permits an 8/4 split for real-time schedule timeline curation versus persistent budget gauge metrics.
  - **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** 8-column layout with `1.5rem` margins and `1rem` gutters. Collapses secondary analytics into lower-stacked segments.
  - **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column layout with `1rem` margins and `0.75rem` gutters. Employs bottom-sheet drawers for budget simulations without losing itinerary scroll position.
- **Rhythm Rules:** All layout flow respects an 8px spatial grid base. Compact components (e.g., budget micro-chips, timeline nodes) use `space-xs` and `space-sm`, while section separations demand `space-lg` to `space-xl` for editorial breathing room.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy rejects excessive blur gradients, relying instead on tonal hospitality layering paired with fine, warm-tinted ambient containment.

- **Surface Tiers:**
  - **Base Layer:** Canvas Warm Ivory (`#FAF8F3`).
  - **Tier 1 (Resting Cards):** Surface Sand (`#F4F0E8`) or Pure White (`#FFFFFF`) with a 1px solid border in `#E5DEC9`.
  - **Tier 2 (Interactive Flyouts & Active Cards):** Pure White (`#FFFFFF`) backed by an ambient, warm-tinted drop shadow: `0 8px 24px -4px rgba(26, 36, 33, 0.08)`.
  - **Tier 3 (Floating Action Bars & Modals):** Pure White (`#FFFFFF`) with deep diffused occlusion: `0 16px 36px -8px rgba(148, 114, 41, 0.16)`.
- **Dividers & Strokes:** Borders act as primary structural dividers. Outlines stay hairline (1px) with low-contrast, earthy pigment values to maintain an organized, paper-planner feel.

## Shapes

The interface embraces tailored roundedness that bridges clean editorial structure with approachable touchpoints:

- **Primary Cards & Containers:** Fixed at 16px corner radii (`rounded-lg` standard within Level 2 configuration), providing an inviting framed look for itinerary checkpoints and daily spending summaries.
- **Buttons & Control Elements:** Configured at 12px corner radii to establish an ergonomic, intentional tactile feel that avoids juvenile pill shapes while remaining comfortable on touch surfaces.
- **Chips, Category Tags & Indicators:** Set to 6px-8px or full pill shapes specifically when used as discrete metadata markers (e.g., `#Sage` lodging tags, currency selectors).
- **Structural Dividers:** Crisp geometric edges with precise 1px line caps maintain architectural discipline.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary CTA:** Muted Taupe (`#877553`) fill with white text, 12px border radius, 48px height for main actions. On hover, shifts to `#746343` with subtle downward elevation.
- **Secondary / Action:** Antique Gold (`#947229`) fill with white text for confirming itinerary revisions and budget locks.
- **Tertiary / Ghost:** Transparent background with 1px border in `#947229`, Antique Gold typography, 12px border radius.

### Cards & Itinerary Modules
- Main itinerary blocks feature a 16px corner radius, white background (`#FFFFFF`), and a 1px border in `#E5DEC9`. 
- Active or currently edited schedule cards introduce an indicator stroke on the leading border in Antique Gold (`#947229`) or Muted Taupe (`#877553`) depending on alert urgency.

### Chips & Badges
- Category badges feature soft pastel fills with high-contrast text:
  - Transport: Sky (`#86C5DA`) with dark teal text.
  - Accommodation: Sage (`#A8C6A0`) with dark forest text.
  - Dining / Leisure: Soft Periwinkle (`#A0B9EF`) with deep bronze text.
- Border radius is set to 8px with tight padding (`4px 8px`).

### Input Fields & Budget Counters
- Form fields adopt a warm ivory-sand background (`#F4F0E8`) in neutral state, shifting to crisp white (`#FFFFFF`) with a 1.5px Antique Gold border on focus.
- Dual-currency balance toggles utilize tabular numeral alignments with fixed width currency glyph slots to prevent layout jitter during live calculations.

### Checkboxes & Segment Selectors
- Custom 20px squares with 6px border radius. Unselected states use a 1.5px stroke in `#E5DEC9`; selected states feature a solid `#947229` fill with a crisp white check icon.

### Trip Coach Metrics Gauge (Specialized Component)
- Horizontal dual-bar visualizer showing 'Planned' vs 'Remaining' budget. The track utilizes `#F4F0E8` with a filled segment in Antique Gold for committed funds and dynamic Muted Taupe for overage warnings.
